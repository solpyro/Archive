Played with a standard 52 card French deck, or t eh 36 card version (missing the 2-5 number cards).

Players are dealt a hand of 6 cards, and another card is revealed as the trump suit.

The first player "attacks" the player to the left by dealing a card and the defender needs to match or beat the dealt card's rank. Then the attacker may launch another attack, restricted to cards of the same rank as any currently played. Each other player around the table has the opportunity to continue the attack when the previous attacker passes, until 6 attacks have been made.

If at any point the defender is unable or unwilling to beat an attacking card, the defender must pick up all the played cards. But if they are successful, the played cards are discarded.

At the end of a round, all players draw back up to 6 cards and the defender becomes the initial attacker for the new round.

Once the deck has been exhausted, players will be eliminated from the game by exhausting their hands. The last player with cards is then named the "durak" (fool)
